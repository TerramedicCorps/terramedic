# Candidate Environmental Organizations

Research compiled for [issue #37](https://github.com/TerramedicCorps/terramedic/issues/37).
These organizations are candidates for evaluation through the Terramedic curation
pipeline — inclusion here does not imply endorsement or final selection.

## Selection Criteria

Organizations were selected to provide:

- Coverage across **SDG 13** (Climate Action), **SDG 14** (Life Below Water),
  and **SDG 15** (Life on Land)
- **Global geographic diversity** — not limited to the US
- A **mix of sizes**: large international, mid-size national, and smaller regional
- Plausible alignment with the 5-step evaluation (mission, transparency,
  accessibility, legitimacy, evidence of work)
